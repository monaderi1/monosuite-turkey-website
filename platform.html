Operating model

Six connected processing stages.

The platform progresses from finding assets to producing management-ready evidence without treating each capability as a separate data island.

Stage 01

Discovery and onboarding

Identify IP-based assets, collect available service and certificate context, then approve, import or manually create managed records.

Stage 02

Inventory and enrichment

Maintain detailed hardware, operating-system, software, service, user, patch, network and routing information.

Stage 03

Change and lifecycle correlation

Compare scan states, preserve unreachable assets and identify active-lifecycle assets that become unreachable.

Stage 04

Relationships and business services

Visualise observed connections and confirm business-service membership with business impact.

Stage 05

Security and control assessment

Assess vulnerabilities, internal exposure and applicable predefined compliance controls.

Stage 06

Scores, dashboards and reporting

Summarise posture while preserving drill-down evidence to asset, control and framework level.
