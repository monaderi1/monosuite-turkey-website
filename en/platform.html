End-to-end model

From discovery to operational action.

01 Discover

Discover and onboard

Use gateway-based adapters for supported network infrastructure, the Cyobik Agent for detailed Windows and Linux inventory, and governed manual or file-import workflows for other assets.

02 Maintain

Maintain asset intelligence

Keep hardware, operating system, software, firmware, users, services, processes, patches, network details and supported asset-specific fields current over time.

03 Relate

Connect relationships and Business Services

Record observed logical or physical relationships, confirm service membership and apply business impact to the assets that support each service.

04 Assess

Evaluate security and exposure

Bring together offline CPE/CVE assessment, Nessus findings, internal attack-surface conditions, protection evidence, compliance results and MITRE ATT&CK context.

05 Prioritise

Explain and prioritise risk

Use asset-level and organisation-level Visibility, Protection and Risk Scores, then drill down to the evidence that contributes to each score.

06 Integrate

Integrate and report

Use dashboards, scheduled reports, named Splunk, Nessus and Jira integrations, and the comprehensive REST API with complete Swagger/OpenAPI documentation.

Governance

Keep access and data boundaries explicit.

Role-based access

Default and custom roles with granular permissions.

Attribute-based restrictions

Limit visible data by maintained attributes such as VLAN, location or business unit.

Auditability

Record user and administrative activity and maintain lower-level system trace information.

Relationship boundary

Cyobik presents observed relationships and confirmed Business Service context. It does not claim to compute a complete universal dependency map for every environment.
