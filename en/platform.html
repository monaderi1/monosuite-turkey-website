END-TO-END CYBER-ASSET MANAGEMENT

From discovery to action: manage the entire process on one platform.

01 Discover

Discover and inventory all your assets

Use gateway-based adapters for supported network infrastructure, the Cyobik Agent for detailed Windows and Linux inventory, and governed manual or file-import workflows for other assets.

02 Maintain

Maintain asset intelligence

Keep hardware, operating system, software, firmware, users, services, running processes, patches and network information continuously up to date. Turn your asset inventory from a point-in-time record into a living source of information that tracks changes across your environment.

03 Relate

Connect relationships and Business Services

Record observed logical or physical relationships, confirm service membership and apply business impact to the assets that support each service.

04 Assess

Assess security posture and gaps in one place

Bring together offline CPE/CVE assessment, Nessus findings, internal attack-surface conditions, protection evidence, compliance results and MITRE ATT&CK context.

05 Prioritise

Explain and prioritise risk

Use Visibility, Protection and Risk Scores to assess risk levels at both asset and organisational level. Examine in detail which assets, security findings and assessment data contribute to each score, so you can set priorities based on reliable data.

06 Integrate

Integrate with your existing systems and report the results

Track your security posture with dashboards and scheduled reports. Integrate Cyobik into your existing operational processes through Splunk, Nessus and Jira integrations; use the comprehensive REST API and Swagger/OpenAPI documentation to develop integrations tailored to your needs.

ACCESS AND DATA MANAGEMENT

You decide who can access which data.

Role-based access

Create default or organisation-specific roles and use granular permissions to control precisely which data and functions users can access.

Access restrictions based on asset attributes

Limit visible assets by maintained attributes such as VLAN, location or business unit, so each team can access only the data within its responsibility and authorisation.

Traceability and audit

Record user and administrative activity and retain system-level traces so changes and access can be reviewed retrospectively.

Asset and Business Service relationships

Cyobik presents observed logical and physical relationships alongside confirmed Business Service context, helping teams understand how assets support operational services.
