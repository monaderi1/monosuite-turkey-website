Scope

This notice applies to visitors who submit a demo request or contact Cyobik through the website. It does not replace customer, contract, employee or supplier privacy notices that may apply to a later relationship.

Data and purposes

What we collect and why.

Data categories

Identity and contact data, including name, work email, company, country and optional phone number; professional data, including optional job title; request data, including evaluation interest and additional details; form metadata, including language, submission time, notice version and source page; and an optional marketing preference.

Processing purposes

To receive and evaluate the demo request, contact the requester, manage pre-contractual and commercial communications, keep a controlled record of the request, protect the form from abuse, and send product or event updates only where separate optional consent has been given.

Legal grounds

Processing needed to take steps at the request of a prospective customer before a contract, compliance with legal obligations where applicable, and Veksacore's legitimate interests in responding to genuine business enquiries and securing the website. Marketing communications are based on separate explicit consent and may be withdrawn at any time.

Collection method

Data is collected directly from the person through the electronic form. Limited technical data may also be generated by website hosting, security and email delivery services while the request is transmitted.

Storage and sharing

Email-only lead handling.

Where the request goes

Demo requests are delivered to and initially stored in the info@veksacore.com mailbox. Veksacore does not currently use a CRM for website demo requests.

Who may receive data

Authorised Veksacore personnel and, only to the extent necessary, website hosting, email delivery, security and infrastructure service providers. Data may also be disclosed to competent public authorities where legally required.

Technical locations

Operational handling is performed by Veksacore in Türkiye. Depending on the technical architecture of website, email and security providers, limited processing may occur in other locations. We do not claim that every technical processing step is confined to Türkiye.

Retention and deletion

Requests are retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond, manage relevant follow-up and meet applicable legal obligations. When the processing purpose and applicable legal grounds cease, the data is deleted, destroyed or anonymised in accordance with applicable requirements. Marketing preferences are retained until consent is withdrawn or the purpose ends.

Security

Access is limited to authorised users and the request is handled through controlled website and email workflows. Do not submit passwords, internal IP addresses, network diagrams, configuration files, vulnerability details or other sensitive security information through the form.

Your rights

You may contact privacy@veksacore.com to exercise your rights under Article 11 of Türkiye's Personal Data Protection Law, including requesting information about processing, correction, deletion or destruction where applicable, and objecting to certain outcomes.
