Keep cyber-asset data and processing inside your environment.

Cyobik runs on virtualisation infrastructure controlled by your organisation. Cyber asset data and analysis remain in your environment, so you can use the platform's full capabilities without moving sensitive security data outside the organisation.

INSTALLATION MODEL

Designed to run in your own infrastructure.

Virtualisation infrastructure

Deploy the supplied OVF package in supported VMware infrastructure.

On-premises operation

Keep the data Cyobik collects and the analysis it performs inside your infrastructure. Only external connections explicitly permitted by your organisation are used.

Fully isolated (air-gapped) operation

Operate Cyobik in fully isolated environments without a permanent internet connection. Keep the platform current through controlled offline update workflows without requiring external connectivity.

UPDATES AND BACKUP

Use the update method that suits your environment.

Updates in connected environments

Where outbound connectivity is permitted, securely retrieve and apply approved Cyobik updates through the supported connection method.

Offline updates

Download approved update packages outside the isolated environment, transfer them into the isolated network through your organisation's secure data-transfer procedures, and apply them through Cyobik.

Configuration and data backup

Back up Cyobik configuration and operational data through supported administration tools to maintain system continuity and enable restoration when needed.

Deploy asset intelligence where your security policy requires it.

Review your virtualisation infrastructure, network architecture, update methods and integration requirements with the Cyobik team to determine the installation model best suited to your organisation.
