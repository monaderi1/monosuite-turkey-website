The problem

Security data is abundant. Defensible context is not.

Scanners, infrastructure tools and security platforms each show part of the picture. Cyobik creates a maintained internal model that ties findings to real assets, business impact and control evidence.

01

Unknown or weakly profiled assets

Discovery results remain difficult to govern when identity, lifecycle and ownership are fragmented.

02

Findings without business context

A vulnerability or exposure does not explain priority until it is connected to asset impact and relationships.

03

Reporting without evidence paths

Executive scores are useful only when teams can drill down to the assets and conditions driving them.

How Cyobik works

From discovery to explainable risk.

Each stage adds verified operational context to a shared asset model.

Discover

Use gateways, supported adapters, endpoint agents, manual creation and file import.

Profile

Maintain hardware, software, services, users, patches, network data and lifecycle context.

Relate

Record observed connections and business-service membership with business impact.

Assess

Evaluate vulnerabilities, internal exposure and predefined compliance controls.

Prioritise

Use Visibility, Protection and Risk Scores with drill-down evidence.

Measurement layer

Three scores, with the evidence underneath.

Visibility score85%

Measures completeness and usability of asset knowledge.

Protection score78%

Evaluates protection posture using supported security, exposure, vulnerability and compliance factors.

Risk score30%

Expresses asset and organisation risk using Impact × Likelihood.

Values are illustrative and do not represent a customer environment.
